# Global Video Policy v1

Create a fully animated cinematic YouTube Short in 9:16 vertical format.

Use only stylized animated environments and animated characters. Do not use photorealistic humans, realistic faces, live-action footage, celebrity likenesses, public-figure likenesses, prominent fictional characters, recognizable copyrighted character designs, logos, trademarks, or imitation of any real person.

Use original role-based animated characters. Keep the same animation medium, rendering style, color palette, lighting, camera language, character design, environment logic, narration voice, pronunciation, and pacing across every scene in the project.

The video must be exactly 10 seconds. Keep spoken narration under 9 seconds and leave the final second for a clean visual hold, transition, or ambient sound. Never end speech mid-word or mid-sentence.

Burn captions into the video, synchronize them accurately, use short mobile-readable lines, and keep captions inside the 9:16 Shorts safe area.
